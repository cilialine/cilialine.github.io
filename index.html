01 / Inertial recalibration Mar – Jul 2025

STRIM-Net: Learning to Recalibrate IMU Signals

Shandong University · Undergraduate Researcher

Extended IMUNet with temporal attention to emphasize informative segments in inertial sequences. Designed comparative experiments across motion scenarios to evaluate accuracy and robustness.

The CV reports a 0.17% ATE improvement on the best-performing dataset. This work contributed to STRIM-Net at ICIA 2025 (third author).

IMU sequencesTemporal attentionRecalibration
02 / Sequence modeling Jul – Sep 2025

IMUNet × Mamba

Shandong University · Project Lead

Investigated the integration of IMUNet and Mamba state-space models for learning from long inertial sequences. Independently designed a lightweight temporal attention module to improve feature representation and computational efficiency.

Supported by the National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program, with Wei serving as project lead.

Inertial featuresMamba / SSMTemporal attention
03 / Multimodal perception Jul – Dec 2025

StateGraphNet

Shandong University · Undergraduate Researcher

Studied a multimodal temporal perception framework combining Mamba2, graph attention networks, and IMUNet. Used state-space modeling for long-range temporal dependencies and graph propagation for structured relationships across sensing modalities.

ATE improvements over the baseline were observed on RONIN-Seen, RONIN-Unseen, RIDI, OxIOD, and the proposed dataset. RA-L manuscript being revised for resubmission; second author.

Sensor modalitiesMamba2 + GATTemporal perception
04 / Field robotics Mar 2026 – Present

Autonomous Mine Inspection

Shandong University · Project Lead

Led an intelligent culvert inspection robot project for mining environments. Developed LiDAR–IMU SLAM and navigation using FAST-LIO for exploration and mapping without a prior map. Integrated YOLO-based crack and water-seepage detection.

The system supports autonomous inspection, obstacle avoidance, and emergency stops. The CV lists a National Second Prize in the Challenge Cup competition.

LiDAR + IMUFAST-LIO / YOLOAutonomous inspection

System design. The solution report organizes the platform around ROS 2, LiDAR–IMU localization, 3D mapping and 2D navigation, multi-view inspection, and traceable defect records. Completed prototype tests and planned competition targets are reported separately.

05 / Neural signal decoding Feb 2026 – Present

EEG-to-Text

UC Berkeley · Undergraduate Researcher · Advisor: Prof. Lexin Li

Investigating computational approaches that connect EEG activity with semantic information for natural-language decoding. Exploring preprocessing, neural feature extraction, and cross-modal representation learning.

Current work focuses on preliminary modeling pipelines and challenges in signal-to-noise ratio, temporal variability, and cross-subject generalization.

EEG signalsCross-modal learningLanguage representations
06 / Control & autonomy Nov 2025 – Jan 2026

UAV Modeling and Intelligent Inspection

Team Technical Project · Leadership Role

Modeled UAV dynamics and designed PID flight control. Applied YOLOv5 to forest patrol imagery and evaluated fire detection across environmental conditions. Built automatic parameter tuning tools with genetic algorithms and particle swarm optimization.

Developed and validated an autonomous forest patrol framework. Code and related data processing are available on GitHub.
